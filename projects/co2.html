Mapping Rover for Indoor Air Quality Monitoring

An autonomous mobile robot designed for indoor environmental monitoring. It utilizes ROS-based navigation, SLAM algorithms, and a custom 3D-printed chassis to traverse indoor spaces, capturing real-time CO₂ data and generating detailed spatial heat maps for air quality analysis.
